## What we make

- **Noekken VPN** for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. The apps and their releases live in [NOEKKEN-LLC/noekken](https://github.com/NOEKKEN-LLC/noekken).
- **Nothing that points to you.** We have no email, name or password on file, so there is nothing about you to leak or sell.
- **Private on any network.** Traffic leaves the device inside an encrypted tunnel; the network you are on sees an encrypted connection, not the sites you open.
- **No leaks while reconnecting.** If the tunnel drops, the app keeps traffic from going around it until it is back.
